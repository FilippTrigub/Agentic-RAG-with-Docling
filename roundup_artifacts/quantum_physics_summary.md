# Quantum Physics Roundup — February 2026

## Key Developments

1. **Macroscopic Schrödinger's Cat States**  
   Researchers have driven thousands of atoms into simultaneous states, pushing superposition to the largest scale yet and tightening the bridge between quantum and classical physics.  
   Source: [LiveScience (Feb 2026)](https://www.livescience.com/physics-mathematics/quantum-physics/physicists-push-thousands-of-atoms-to-a-schrodingers-cat-state-bringing-the-quantum-world-closer-to-reality-than-ever-before)

2. **Hidden Geometry in Quantum Materials**  
   University of Geneva scientists uncovered geometric structures inside quantum materials that bend electron paths analogously to gravity, enabling new control of conductivity and photonic interactions.  
   Source: [ScienceDaily (Jan 2026)](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/01/260131084616.htm)

3. **Entanglement vs. Black Holes**  
   New theoretical work suggests quantum entanglement signatures might survive even after one particle crosses a black-hole event horizon, challenging assumptions about information loss.  
   Source: [The Quantum Insider (Jan 2026)](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/01/03/even-black-holes-may-not-fully-hide-quantum-entanglement/)

4. **Quantum Computing Momentum**  
   - Multi-objective optimization benchmarks show tangible progress toward practical applications.  
   - Superconducting microwave cavities achieved 14.6 dB of intracavity squeezing via weak Kerr nonlinearity.  
   - Modular silicon-based processors with 11 phosphorus-atom qubits demonstrate scalable architectures.  
   Sources: [Nature Portfolio Quantum Physics Collection](https://www.nature.com/subjects/quantum-physics)

5. **Outlook for 2026**  
   PhysicsWorld forecasts rapid advances across quantum materials, matter waves, quantum information, metrology, and single-photon effects—driving opportunities in electronics, sensing, and fundamental physics.  
   Source: [PhysicsWorld (Jan 2026)](https://physicsworld.com/a/happy-new-year-so-what-will-happen-in-physics-in-2026/)

## Summary
Quantum physics in early 2026 is defined by scaling quantum phenomena to macroscopic regimes, unveiling geometric control in materials, probing entanglement near black holes, and maturing quantum computing hardware. Together, these breakthroughs signal accelerating convergence between theoretical insights and application-ready quantum technologies.
